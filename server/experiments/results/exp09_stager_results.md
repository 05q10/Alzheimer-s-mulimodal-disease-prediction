- **FreeSurfer MRI Volumes**: On the exact subset of test subjects with observed MRI ($N=1996$ visits), adding volumetric features (T2b -> T4) changes Macro-F1 by **+0.0267** (95% Cluster-Bootstrap CI: `[0.008, 0.046]`) and changes mean predictive entropy by **+0.0175**.

- **Clinical Interpretation**: Syndromic stage (clinical dementia rating and mild cognitive impairment) is by medical definition established primarily from cognitive and functional impairment. While CSF and MRI biomarkers are essential for **biological etiology** (ruling out non-AD causes and establishing amyloid/tau status) and for **longitudinal progression risk** (Model B), their incremental diagnostic value for purely determining *current syndromic stage* once high-quality cognitive batteries (CDR-SB + ADAS11 + MMSE) are already in hand is modest to minimal.
