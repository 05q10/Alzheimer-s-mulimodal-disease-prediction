## 1. Executive Summary & Honest Clinical Assessment

### Do CSF and MRI Add Anything Beyond Noise Over Cognitive Tests for Staging?

**Honest Answer**: **For cross-sectional syndromic diagnosis (CN vs MCI vs AD), cognitive batteries (MMSE, CDR-SB, ADAS11) dominate the prediction.**

- **CSF Biomarkers (ABETA/TAU/PTAU)**: On the exact subset of test subjects with observed CSF ($N=486$ visits), adding CSF to full cognitive scores (T2b -> T3) changes Macro-F1 by **+0.0135** (95% Cluster-Bootstrap CI: `[-0.0009, 0.032]`) and changes mean predictive entropy by **+0.0432**.
  - *Empirical finding*: The 95% cluster-bootstrap CI for Delta Macro-F1 spans zero. CSF does not statistically significantly improve cross-sectional syndromic staging accuracy over a comprehensive cognitive battery.
  - *Possible explanation (not tested in this observational analysis)*: Preclinical amyloid and tau alterations frequently occur in cognitively normal individuals and can plateau prior to dementia onset, introducing ambiguity into syndromic labels.

- **Isolated FreeSurfer MRI Volumes (T2b -> T2b+MRI, no CSF)**: On the exact subset of test subjects with observed MRI ($N=1996$ visits), adding volumetric features strictly without CSF changes Macro-F1 by **+0.0201** (95% Cluster-Bootstrap CI: `[0.0014, 0.0396]`) and changes mean predictive entropy by **+0.0192**.
  - *Empirical finding*: FreeSurfer volumes provide a modest, statistically detectable boost in Macro-F1 with lower predictive entropy.
  - *Possible explanation (not tested in this observational analysis)*: Macroscopic hippocampal and entorhinal atrophy may provide objective anatomical confirmation in borderline MCI vs early AD cases where functional test scores alone are ambiguous.

- **Clinical Interpretation**: Syndromic stage is clinically defined by cognitive and functional status. Biomarkers are essential for longitudinal risk modeling (Model B) and biological diagnosis, but their incremental value for *purely classifying current cross-sectional syndromic stage* once comprehensive cognitive batteries are available is modest to minimal.
